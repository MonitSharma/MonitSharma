### About

I am a research engineer working at the intersection of quantum computing, optimization, and machine learning. My work spans quantum–classical algorithms, numerical methods, and AI systems applied to scientific problems.

I care about reproducible research, careful benchmarking, and building software that makes advanced methods practical to run, evaluate, and teach — bridging the gap between published theory and dependable implementations.
