### About

I am a research engineer working at the intersection of **quantum computing, optimization, and machine learning**. My work spans quantum–classical algorithms, numerical methods, and AI systems applied to scientific problems.

I care about reproducible research, careful benchmarking, and building software that turns published theory into dependable, practical implementations — code that is easy to run, evaluate, and teach.

Alongside research, I write and maintain open educational material to make quantum computing and scientific computing more approachable.
